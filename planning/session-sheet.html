The task — say it, then stay quiet

"Think out loud; I'll stay quiet."
"Test whether a simple rule would have made money on a stock you know."

Don't help. When they get stuck, write down where and wait 30 seconds before saying anything. Where they stick is the finding.

The flow they should reach (for your reference — don't read it to them)

  1. Sign up — free account
  2. Land on the Dashboard (the workspace)
  3. Type a ticker they know (AAPL, TSLA, RELIANCE…)
  4. Pick a strategy: Momentum / MACD / Bollinger — leave the numbers at default
  5. Click Run backtest (~10s first time)
  6. Read the result — did $1 grow vs just holding?
  7. Open the Validation tab → hits the Pro wall (intentional)
