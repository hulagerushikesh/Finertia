I tested 129 simple trading strategies on the Nifty 50. Four survived honest
testing, and all four were on stocks that were collapsing.

The setup: three textbook strategies (momentum, MACD, Bollinger bands) on
every stock in the Nifty 50 as it stood in January 2011, fifteen years of
prices, a 0.1% cost on every trade. Each one tuned on the first 70% of its
history, then judged on years the tuning never saw.

The funnel:
→ 129 tested
→ 50 beat simply holding the stock on the years they were tuned on
→ 7 were still ahead on the unseen years
→ 4 passed three checks for luck

All four survivors were on Reliance Communications, Reliance Infrastructure
and Reliance Power. They didn't find good moments to buy. They beat holding
because they were out of the market for most of a long decline.

The second half was worse for the tuner. On Reliance Industries I tried 834
settings. None beat holding the stock, on either stretch of years. The best
on the tuning years ranked 623rd of 834 on the unseen ones.

I wrote the rules down before running anything, and every figure is re-run
by a script anyone can check.
